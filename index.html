          Choose your upcoming TCG releases here. We’ll guide you through confirmation, payment, and updates in Messenger.

          On our website · Step 1

          Choose your items

          Add preorder items to your crate. You'll see the 30% downpayment for each one.

          On our website · Step 2

          Enter your Facebook Name and tap checkout

          Use the name on your Facebook profile so we can match your order to your Messenger chat. Then pick one:

          Assisted checkout

          Your order text is copied for you. Messenger opens. Paste the order and send it.

          Self-checkout (QR)

          Pay the deposit using the QR code. Then send your order screenshot and proof of payment in Messenger.

          Both options end here · Step 3

          Send it to our Facebook page inbox

          Whichever checkout you pick, your order and proof of payment go to our Facebook page inbox. Facebook only lets us reply after you message us first, so sending your order starts the chat.

          In our Facebook page inbox · Step 4

          We confirm your deposit

          We check your payment and reply in the chat to confirm your order.

          In our Facebook page inbox · Step 5

          We update you on allocation

          Distributors decide allocation after orders are placed. We message you once your final quantity is confirmed.

          In our Facebook page inbox · Step 6

          Pay the balance and get shipping updates

          When your items arrive, we message you the remaining balance. After you pay, we send shipping updates in the same chat.

          Keep your Messenger on

          All updates on your preorder come from our Facebook page. Message us from the same Facebook account you used for your order so we can find it fast.
